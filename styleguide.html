Component 09

FaqItem — clustered

Questions cluster under a titled heading; items reuse the live .faq-list details styling, so clustered and flat FAQs are one component.

The engine

Does Lookauramax use AI to rate my face?

Every band comes from clear, explainable checks on your photo. No learned attractiveness model, no face rating — every score shows its why.

What if my photo is too dark or blurry?

Before any signal is read, a photo-quality gate checks brightness, sharpness and contrast. If the photo cannot support an honest read, that signal is marked “Not assessed” with the fix named — never given a low band.

Component 12

AnswerBlock

The answer-first box articles and tools open with: the direct answer leads, in full sentences a reader — or an answer engine — can quote as-is.

How does AuraScan score my photo?

It doesn’t score your face. Clear, explainable checks — no learned score, no face rating, ever. Each of the six traits gets a band — Focus first, Good or Strong — with the Why and the Action attached.

Low confidence means no band, not a low band: if the photo cannot support an honest read, the signal is marked “Not assessed” and the fix is named.
