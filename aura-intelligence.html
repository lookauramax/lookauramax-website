How AuraScan works

Aura Intelligence is Lookauramax's analysis engine — the clear, explainable checks behind AuraScan, the app's photo read. It is not a learned model, and it never rates a face. AuraScan runs 15 individual checks: the photo-quality gate first, then 12 measurements on your face photo, with posture read from a guided side photo. The six traits — skin (one signal, two reads: clarity and glow), hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, and posture — are placed into bands, not numbers, and every band shows its Why, its Action, and its confidence.

The gate comes first

Before a single signal is scored, the photo itself is checked. On the face photo, that starts with the gate measurements. 12 individual measurements on your photo — texture, colour, light and sharpness, region by region — behind your six trait bands.

The gate reads three measurements:

  • Average brightness — the overall light level of the photo. It decides gating and confidence only; by design it never raises or lowers a signal's band, so a dark room never counts against your skin.
  • Sharpness — the focus and steadiness of the photo: how strong the edges in the frame are, relative to its brightness.
  • Overall contrast — the spread of brightness across the whole photo: whether the frame contains real structure at all, or is a blank wall.

If a photo cannot support a fair read of a signal — too dark, too much glare, or too blurred — that signal is shown as Not assessed, with the fix named, and you still get a baseline routine for it. If the whole photo fails — no face-like structure, far too dark, blown out, or far too blurred — the scan stops and asks for a retake instead. Low confidence means no score, never a low one.

The 15 checks, in full

15 individual checks behind one scan — before a single band is assigned. That is the full scan: the face photo, the beard read if you opt in, and a guided side photo for posture. Every check the engine runs is listed below, grouped by the signal it feeds. Nothing else is measured, and nothing on this list is estimated or rounded up.

Each check measures a region of the photo frame — the centre, the top band, the brow band, the lower-centre band. These are frame-region estimates, not facial mapping: AuraScan does not locate facial landmarks on your face photo, and the regions are approximations of where each area usually sits in a framed photo.

Bands, not numbers

Every assessed signal gets one of three bands. There is no number, because a single number would claim a precision these checks do not have.

  • Focus first — this is the signal to attend to first. It leads your plan, because a steady routine will do the most good here.
  • Good — this signal is holding up well. The plan maintains it with the standard routine.
  • Strong — this signal is in a strong place on these checks. The plan keeps what is working and turns attention elsewhere.

Each band also carries a confidence level. High means a clean photo with nothing gated. Medium means part of the photo was gated or weak — dim light or soft sharpness — so the band should be read with that in mind. If confidence would be low, there is no band at all: the signal is Not assessed, or the scan asks for a retake.

From read to plan

15 checks on your photos, then 10 personal inputs — your goals, your time, your sleep, your scan itself — shape the plan you get. The ten inputs:

  • Your goals — the focus areas you choose select the goal-based tasks in your routine.
  • Your daily time budget — sets how many tasks each day carries, and the plan's daily minutes.
  • Your top priority area — that area's task leads each day's routine, ahead of scan order.
  • Your current routine level — starting from none softens Week 1, so the plan begins where you actually are; the Day 1 starter is never softened.
  • Your sleep — short sleep adds an evening wind-down task.
  • Your exercise — regular exercise swaps the starter walk for a mobility task.
  • Your journey length — 90, 180 or 365 days, or ongoing: sets the plan's Aura Cycles and which check-in milestones are drawn.
  • The scan itself — your lowest bands lead the routine; signals that were not assessed get honest baseline tasks instead, and signals you switch off are excluded entirely.
  • Products you already own — the matching task names your own product, so the routine works with what is already on your shelf.
  • Weekly-review adaptation — your weekly answer — too easy, right, or too much — adds or removes one task a day for the next week.

One of the ten inputs is the scan itself, which is why the two counts are always named separately on this site, and never added together into a single bigger number.

What the detection steps do

Your bands are produced by clear, explainable checks — measurements on texture, colour, light and sharpness, listed in full on this page. Two supporting steps use your phone's standard on-device detection: it guides your framing while you take the photo, and it reads your posture from the side photo. Detection never scores your face, and no learned model rates how you look.

Limits, stated plainly

  • The checks are frame-region estimates, not facial mapping. There are no facial landmarks on your face photo, and no measurement isolates skin or hair anatomy — the regions are approximations, and a photo that frames you off-centre can mislead them.
  • A photo can mislead in other ways too: heavy beauty smoothing can leave too little real detail to read, and harsh light can gate a signal. The gate exists so those photos are re-taken or marked Not assessed, not scored unfairly.
  • Smile is a rough guide from photo signals only. Posture needs the side photo. Neither is a medical read, and Lookauramax does not diagnose or treat any medical, skin, dental or mental-health condition.
  • Nothing here rates attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age or race — nothing you cannot act on is ever scored.

Questions, answered

Does Lookauramax use AI?

No. Every band comes from clear, explainable checks on your photo. No learned attractiveness model, no face rating — every score shows its why. Two supporting steps use your phone's standard on-device detection — framing guidance while you take the photo, and the posture read from your side photo — and detection never scores your face.

How many checks does AuraScan run?

15 individual checks behind one scan — before a single band is assigned. Twelve of them run on the face photo; the other three read posture from a guided side photo, and every check is listed in full in the tables on this page.

Why did my scan ask for a retake?

Because the photo-quality gate runs first, and your photo could not be read fairly — too dark, blown out with glare, too blurred, or without face-like structure in frame. AuraScan asks for a better photo instead of guessing: an unreadable signal is marked Not assessed or re-taken, never given a low score.

Does Lookauramax rate attractiveness?

No. Lookauramax never scores attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, or anything you cannot act on. It reads actionable signals only, reports them as bands with a Why and an Action, and states its limits on the limitations page.
