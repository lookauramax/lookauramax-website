Changelog

What changed on this website, and when. Only changes that actually shipped are listed here — nothing announced before it is live.

Addendum, same day: one poster-story description that was cut off mid-word without an ellipsis mark — missed by the sweep that keyed on the ellipsis — has been completed from the page's own words. A whole-tree check now confirms every description on the site ends in a complete sentence.

23 October 2026 — Images, contrast, and structured-data checks

  • Every content image now loads as WebP first. 2,363 image tags across the site were converted to the picture pattern our articles already used — modern browsers download the WebP (about 60% lighter), older browsers still get the JPG. The 19 images that had no WebP version were encoded at the same quality standard as the existing set. Social-card and structured-data images stay JPG, as platforms prefer.
  • Muted text is brighter. The smallest grey text tone failed contrast checks on our dark panels; it is now a lighter shade of the same warm grey (5.75:1 on black, up from 4.74:1), so small print is easier to read everywhere.
  • A semantic structured-data check joins our site tools. Our lint already proved every JSON-LD block parses; the new check also proves the value types are what Google expects (numbers as numbers, dates in ISO format, absolute URLs). It passes on all 369 pages — including the five pages Search Console flagged from an older crawl.
  • Blog index completed: the Diwali and Chhath guides now have their cards (they were missing), the hair-washing guide card sits with the guides instead of the poster stories, and the post-count line adds up exactly.
  • Framing completed: the eyebrow guide is now written for men throughout, and the friendship guide no longer carries a teen section or teen FAQ — the site and the app are 18+.

23 October 2026 — Reading order, descriptions, and framing pass

  • Heading order fixed on every page. Article pages used to show the "In this guide" contents label as a heading before the page title; it is now a styled label, and the homepage's signal labels follow the same rule — every page now reads in proper order for search engines and screen readers.
  • 143 page descriptions completed. Poster-story descriptions that used to end mid-sentence now end in complete sentences, written from each page's own words — nothing invented.
  • 42 long page titles shortened so they display in full in search results, with each page's topic words kept.
  • Blog index: the flagship Complete Men's Glow-Up Guide now has a featured slot at the top, and two different poster stories that shared one title are now told apart.
  • Framing aligned: the friendship guide is now framed for young adults (the app is 18+), and the conversation guide states plainly that it is the mirror of the men's guide.

23 October 2026 — The Monthly Plan page

  • A new page: The Monthly Plan. "Monthly plan" means two things, so the page answers both — the current month's dated grooming plan (November 2026: the haircut window, Diwali week, Movember, wedding season, the winter skin shift) and the Monthly plan itself, priced openly next to the 3-Month and Yearly plans.
  • The 90-Day Planner and the 90-day glow-up guide now show your three months inside the cycle — Month 1 Foundation (Days 1–30), Month 2 Building (Days 31–60), Month 3 Refining (Days 61–90) — the planner with real calendar dates from your start day.
  • Two shortcuts now land where people expect: /pricing goes to the pricing guide, and /faq goes to the homepage FAQ. The comparison page no longer promises a starting point "in under a minute" — an unmeasured claim for an app that has not launched.

23 October 2026 — A steadier homepage hero

  • On desktop, the homepage hero could jump while the page loaded, because one display font arrived late. That font is now sent with the page itself, and the hero poster's space is reserved from the start, so the hero holds still while it loads.

23 October 2026 — Round 4 truth release

  • Photo quality is a gate, never a scored trait. Every list of the six traits on the site now ends with that sentence in the same words — and a lint rule in our site tools keeps it true.
  • One per-month rule everywhere: the plan price divided by its months, rounded down, never up. The homepage and the pricing guide now agree exactly, in all 10 launch markets.
  • The scan is two photos, not one: a face photo, plus a guided side photo for posture. Every page that said "one photo" now says so — including the shared block on 322 posts.
  • Comparison tables now lead with the free scan (no plan, no card) instead of denying that a free scan exists, and the About page states the launch age rule plainly: 18 and over; a Minors Mode for ages 16–17 is planned for a later release.
  • Corrected the visible counts (325 posts — 85 guides and 240 poster stories), the Diwali guide's dates, and the journal pages' structured data, which had been naming the blog index instead of themselves.

23 October 2026 — Answer pages, tightened

  • A new page: Lookauramax reviews — the honest status. The app has not launched, so no genuine user reviews or ratings exist anywhere yet; the page says so plainly, and says where real reviews will live after launch.
  • The methodology hub now opens with a plain definition of Aura Intelligence, and our AuraScan guide leads with the gate, the 12 measurements on your photo, and the 15 checks in the full scan.
  • The homepage FAQ now gives US prices alongside India prices, carries the full answer on AI and face rating, and answers "Are there any Lookauramax reviews?" directly.
  • Our glow-up apps comparison carried a 6-month price in the Lookauramax column — a plan that does not exist. Corrected: Monthly, 3-Month, and Yearly only. The same table now states plainly that photo quality is a gate, never scored.
  • The limitations page now answers "Does Lookauramax rate your face?" in so many words — no, never — and its introduction counts the six traits correctly.

23 October 2026 — Round 3 fixes

  • One header, everywhere: the floating capsule header now renders identically on every page — homepage, blog, tools, glossary, and hubs. Legacy full-width bar rules and a wordmark size difference on inner pages were removed, and the header stylesheets are cache-versioned so the fix cannot go stale.
  • The homepage was rebuilt: a proof strip with the counted checks (15 in the full scan, 12 on your photo, 10 personal inputs), a "What free includes" section, the Analysis Lens moved after the signals it reads, and clear pre-launch markers on pricing — the launch list is the way to join until launch day.
  • One counting story everywhere: six traits receive bands; photo quality is a gate, checked first and never scored.
  • The Privacy Policy, Terms, Refund Policy, and Parent Consent pages moved onto the standard site design. Their policy text is byte-for-byte unchanged — only the page shell changed, and each page now shows its policy-text date and the design-update date.
  • Our two overlapping glow-up guides were merged into one — The Complete Men's Glow-Up Guide — and the old address redirects to it.
  • llms.txt was refreshed (free-scan facts, current counts, India-first pricing), and the organisation logo in structured data is now the coin.

23 October 2026 — Daily Answers begins

  • A new daily series answers one real, searched men's-grooming question per day, researched and written to the same standard as our guides. First answer published: How often should men wash their hair?

23 October 2026 — Header and hero upgrade

  • The capsule header gained the larger coin logo (which turns over on a slow loop), the tagline beneath the wordmark, and larger hero buttons on the homepage.

23 October 2026 — Chhath Puja grooming guide

  • Published: Chhath Puja grooming — look fresh across four days.

22 October 2026 — Round 2 rebuild

  • The pure-black redesign went live: "The Read" hero, the capsule header, and the returning marquee of Aura names with a pause control.
  • The methodology hub (Aura Intelligence) and the limitations page — what Lookauramax cannot tell you — were published.

20 October 2026 — Plan v2 website pass

  • Result bands renamed everywhere at once: Focus first, Good, Strong. The previous entry-level band label is retired.
  • The plan lineup was simplified to three plans: Monthly, 3-Month (Recommended · One Full 90-Day Cycle), and Yearly (Best Savings Per Month). The half-year plan was retired.
  • The one-time joining reward was removed from the entire website and replaced by the launch list: one email on launch day.
  • The homepage was restructured into nine sections, from fourteen.
  • Six topic hubs were published — Skincare, Hair & Grooming, Style, Fitness & Wellness, Confidence, and The Glow-Up — and every poster story now links up to its parent guide.
  • Every Aura Tool gained a methodology section: what it calculates, its rules and assumptions, and its limitations.
  • This changelog and the Limitations page were published.
  • Headings moved to Cormorant Garamond, matching the app.

20 October 2026 — Consistency pass

  • Pricing labels normalised across the site: the 3-Month plan is “Recommended”, the Yearly plan is “Best Savings Per Month”, and no other wording is used.
  • Structured data (JSON-LD) normalised and validated across all 336 pages; titles and descriptions aligned to the same facts.

17 October 2026 — lookauramax.com launched

  • The website went live: honest guides, twelve Aura Tools, the Glow-Up Glossary, and the legal pages.
