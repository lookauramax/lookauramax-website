Lookauramax — Terms of Service

Important: Prepared without a lawyer, to the best of our ability. NOT legal advice. NOT reviewed by a lawyer. Residual legal risk remains.

1. What you agree to

By using Lookauramax, you agree to these Terms and to the Privacy Policy at https://lookauramax.com/privacy. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

2. Who can use the app

  • You must be 16 or older.
  • Ages 16–17 need a parent/guardian's consent before paid access and before a scan.
  • You must join with Google or with an email magic link. There is no guest mode.
  • One account per person. You are responsible for keeping your account secure.

3. What the app does (and does not do)

  • Lookauramax provides general wellness, grooming, skincare, posture, fitness, sleep, style, and photo guidance. It scores ONLY actionable traits (skin, hair, brows, beard if you opt in, smile, posture, photo quality).
  • It does NOT rate attractiveness, score bone structure, diagnose or treat any condition, prescribe anything, recommend surgery, promise any result, or promise any weight change.
  • For information only, not medical advice. Lookauramax does not diagnose or treat any condition. For skin, health, or mental-health concerns, consult a qualified professional.
  • Spot-reduction is a myth. The app never claims face exercises, mewing, chewing, face yoga, or jaw exercisers give a jawline or burn face fat. Jawline visibility depends on overall body fat, genetics, and bone — which the app never scores or changes.
  • Scan results are rule-based estimates, not AI. Coach guidance is rule-based. Preview is an enhancement-based illustration, labelled "illustration of potential, not a guarantee".

4. Your photo

  • You may only scan your own face. Do not scan other people's faces without their consent.
  • Your photo is processed on your device and wiped after scoring. It is never uploaded. Before/After photos are opt-in, on-device only, encrypted, and deletable.

5. Join Reward and Pro (Trial-Only)

There is no permanent Free plan, no Weekly plan, and no Lifetime plan. The only free thing is the one-time Join Reward: 1 scan + 1 retake if low-confidence + a 3-day routine + a Preview teaser + a Library teaser, no card required. After it is used, you need a paid plan (starting with a free trial) to continue.

Pro (paid via Google Play Billing): full plan, unlimited rescans, full Preview, Aura Coach, full library, Style Lab guidance, full progress with 30-day tracking, reminders, streaks, and badges.
