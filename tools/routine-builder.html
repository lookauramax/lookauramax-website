Consistency beats complexity. A 3-step routine you do every day outperforms a 10-step routine you abandon in a week. Start simple, make it a habit, and only add a step once the basics are automatic. Give any routine 4–6 weeks before judging it — skin and hair change slowly.

Frequently asked questions

How many steps should a daily routine have?

As few as you will actually do every day. A simple routine — cleanse, moisturise, sunscreen in the morning; cleanse, moisturise at night — beats a 10-step routine you quit in a week. Add steps only after the basics are a habit.

Do I need different products for morning and night?

Not necessarily different products, but different jobs: mornings are about protection (sunscreen), nights are about cleansing the day off and moisturising. This builder keeps the steps general — it never recommends specific products.

How long before a routine shows results?

Skin and hair habits take weeks, not days. Give a simple routine at least 4–6 weeks of consistency before judging it. Switching products every few days makes it impossible to tell what helped.
