What your result means

Hair type is a spectrum, and many people sit between two categories — that is normal. The result is a starting point: it mainly tells you how much moisture your hair likely needs, how often it needs washing, and how gently it should be handled. It does not say anything about how good your hair is. Every hair type can look healthy with the right habits.

If your hair changed suddenly — new texture, patches of loss, heavy shedding, or scalp pain — that is worth a dermatologist visit rather than a new shampoo. Sudden changes can have medical causes, and a quiz cannot check for them.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this hair type quiz?

It is a general guide, not a measurement. Hair type exists on a spectrum and many people sit between categories. Use the result as a starting point for care habits, not as a label you must fit exactly.

Can hair type change over time?

Yes. Age, hormones, heat damage, chemical treatments, medication, and health changes can all shift texture over time. If your hair changes suddenly or you notice heavy shedding, a dermatologist can check for underlying causes.

What does my hair type change about care?

Mainly washing frequency, moisture needs, and how much heat or manipulation your hair tolerates. Curlier hair usually needs more moisture and gentler handling; straighter hair usually gets oily faster and may need more frequent washing.

Do I need special products for my hair type?

No. A gentle shampoo, conditioner suited to how dry your hair feels, and heat protection when you style cover most needs. This quiz does not recommend specific products — habits matter more than brands.

Disclaimer: This quiz gives general information only. It does not diagnose scalp or hair conditions and does not recommend medical treatment. For sudden hair loss, scalp pain, or persistent scalp conditions, speak to a dermatologist.
