Free Habit Streak Tracker

By Lookauramax Team · Published 17 Oct 2026 · General information, not medical advice.

An Aura Streak is the number of consecutive days you checked off a habit. Add up to 5 habits, tap to check each one off, and watch your Aura Streak grow. No account is needed. Start below.

What is the Habit Streak Tracker?

The Habit Streak Tracker is a free tool that tracks up to five daily habits. Each completed day adds to your Aura Streak. Missed days are normal. The tracker restarts the next day without guilt. One missed day is a pause, not a failure.

How to use this tool

  1. Type a habit name and select Add habit.
  2. Select Mark done today for a habit you finished.
  3. Return each day to check off the habit again.
  4. Watch your Aura Streak grow with each consecutive day.

No sign-up needed — the tool works right here in your browser.

How to make Aura Streaks actually work

Pick habits so small you cannot fail them. "Ten-minute walk" beats "one hour at the gym." "Wash face" beats "full 8-step routine." An Aura Streak works because it makes skipping visible — one missed day costs you the number you were proud of. But a broken streak is not a failure: the goal is to start a new one the next day, not to punish yourself for the miss.

Keep the list short. Five habits is the maximum here for a reason — most people who try to track ten habits quit all of them by week two. One or two habits done every day for a month will change more than ten habits done for three days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account for the habit tracker?

No. Add habits and check them off with no account. Your habits are saved in this browser, so they are still here when you return on the same device and browser. Clearing your browser data removes them. Use the Clear all data button to delete everything at any time.

How is an Aura Streak counted?

An Aura Streak is the number of consecutive days up to today on which you checked off the habit. If you miss a day, the Aura Streak resets to zero — that is normal, and the goal is to start a new Aura Streak the next day.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your habits and check-ins are removed, because the tracker keeps them in this browser with no backup. Add your habits again to start fresh.

How many habits can I track?

Up to 5 habits. Fewer, well-chosen habits work better than a long list — start with one or two you can actually do every day, then add more once they feel easy.
