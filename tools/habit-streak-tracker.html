How to make streaks actually work

Pick habits so small you cannot fail them. "Ten-minute walk" beats "one hour at the gym." "Wash face" beats "full 8-step routine." A streak works because it makes skipping visible — one missed day costs you the number you were proud of. But a broken streak is not a failure: the goal is to start a new one the next day, not to punish yourself for the miss.

Keep the list short. Five habits is the maximum here for a reason — most people who try to track ten habits quit all of them by week two. One or two habits done every day for a month will change more than ten habits done for three days.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my habit data stored?

Only in this browser, using localStorage on your own device. Nothing is sent to any server, no account is created, and no one else can see your habits. Use the Clear all data button to delete everything at any time.

How is a streak counted?

A streak is the number of consecutive days up to today on which you checked off the habit. If you miss a day, the streak resets to zero — that is normal, and the goal is to start a new streak the next day.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your habits and check-ins are deleted, because they live only in this browser. The tracker has no backup and no sync, by design — your data never leaves your device.

How many habits can I track?

Up to 5 habits. Fewer, well-chosen habits work better than a long list — start with one or two you can actually do every day, then add more once they feel easy.
