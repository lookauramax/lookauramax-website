Free 90-Day Glow-Up Planner

By Lookauramax Team · Published 17 Oct 2026 · General information, not medical advice.

Ninety days is long enough to build real habits and short enough to stay focused. Pick a start date below and get your milestone dates — Day 30, 60 and 90 — plus a weekly checklist for one Aura Cycle. Each milestone is an Aura Check-in: a review point, not a judgement day.

What is the 90-Day Planner?

The 90-Day Planner is a free tool that maps one Aura Cycle into Day 30, 60, and 90 milestones. It gives you a weekly checklist for the cycle. It helps you plan the whole cycle before it starts. No sign-up is needed.

How to use this tool

  1. Pick your start date.
  2. Select Build my 90-day plan to see your milestone dates.
  3. Read your Day 30, 60 and 90 Aura Check-in dates.
  4. Follow the weekly checklist and review at each milestone.

How to use the plan

Each week has the same three jobs: do your routine, check in honestly, and adjust one thing if something is not working. Milestone days (Day 30, 60, 90) are review points — a good moment to re-check your routine, compare a photo taken in the same light, and decide what to keep or change. Missed days are normal; the plan does not reset, and there is no penalty for an imperfect week.

Keep the plan realistic. A routine you actually do six days a week beats a perfect routine you abandon in week two. If a habit keeps failing, shrink it until it is easy — a two-minute version done daily is worth more than an ambitious version done never.

Frequently asked questions

How does the 90-day planner work?

Pick a start date. The planner shows your Day 1, Day 30, Day 60 and Day 90 milestone dates, plus a weekly checklist. No sign-up is needed.

Will I see results in 90 days?

No tool can promise that. 90 days is a useful planning horizon for building habits — skincare, grooming, posture, sleep, exercise — but visible change depends on your starting point, consistency, and factors you cannot control. The planner helps you stay consistent; it does not guarantee an outcome.

What should I do on milestone days?

Use them as review points, not judgement days. Re-check your routine, note what you actually stuck to, adjust one or two things, and keep going. Comparing a fresh photo in the same light can help you see gradual change more honestly than the mirror.

What if I miss days?

Missed days are normal. The plan does not reset and there is no penalty — Pick up the next day. Consistency over 90 days matters far more than a perfect streak.
