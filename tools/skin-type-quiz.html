Find Your Skin Type

By Lookauramax Team · Last updated: 17 Oct 2026 · General information, not medical advice.

Skin type describes how your skin usually behaves — dry, oily, combination, or sensitive-leaning. Answer 6 questions about how your skin feels and looks, and this quiz gives you your likely type plus a care checklist. Start below.

What is the Skin Type Quiz?

The Skin Type Quiz is a free tool that asks six questions about your skin. It suggests your likely skin type and a care checklist. It is a guide, not a diagnosis. Your routine follows from your type. Answer honestly for the most useful result.

How to use this tool

  1. Read each question and choose the answer that fits your skin most days.
  2. Answer all 6 questions.
  3. Select See my skin type to see your likely skin type.
  4. Read your care checklist and the next steps.

0 of 6 answered

1. How does your skin feel by midday?

2. How visible are your pores?

3. After washing your face with lukewarm water (no products), how does it feel after 30 minutes?

4. How often does your skin react to new products or weather changes?

5. How does your skin look in photos taken in natural light?

6. How does moisturizer usually feel on your skin?

No sign-up needed — the tool works right here in your browser.

What to do with your result

Your skin type is a starting point, not a verdict. The essentials are the same for everyone: gentle cleansing, a moisturizer that suits how your skin feels, and daily sunscreen. The differences are in texture and strength — dry skin usually wants richer creams, oily skin usually wants lighter gels, and sensitive-leaning skin wants fewer products and a patch test first.

Introduce one new product at a time, and stop anything that stings, burns, or causes a rash. Skin also changes with seasons, climate, age, and health — re-check every few months instead of treating one result as permanent.

Frequently asked questions

What are the main skin types?

The common categories are dry, oily, combination, and sensitive-leaning. Combination means oily in some zones (often the T-zone) and normal or dry elsewhere. Sensitive-leaning skin reacts easily to products, weather, or friction.

Can my skin type change over time?

Yes. Age, seasons, hormones, climate, products, and health can all shift how your skin behaves. Re-checking every few months is normal — a quiz result is a snapshot, not a permanent label.

Is this quiz a diagnosis?

No. This quiz gives general information based on how you describe your skin. It does not diagnose acne, eczema, rosacea, or any skin condition. Persistent or painful skin problems need a dermatologist.

What should I do after learning my skin type?

Start with the essentials for your type: gentle cleansing, moisturizer suited to how your skin feels, and daily sunscreen. Introduce one new product at a time and stop anything that stings, burns, or causes a rash.
