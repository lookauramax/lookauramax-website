Frequently asked questions

How do I convert cm to feet and inches?

Divide cm by 2.54 to get total inches, then divide by 12 for feet. The remainder is inches. For example, 175 cm is 68.9 inches, which is 5 feet 9 inches. This converter does it immediately.

How do I convert kg to lbs?

Multiply kg by 2.20462 to get pounds. For example, 70 kg is about 154.3 lbs. To go back, divide lbs by 2.20462.

Is this converter accurate?

Yes — it uses the exact definitions: 1 inch = 2.54 cm and 1 kg = 2.20462 lbs. Results are rounded for readability.

Do I need to sign up to use this converter?

No. The tool works right here in your browser — no account, no name or email needed.
