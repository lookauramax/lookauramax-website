Frequently asked questions

Are Aura Tools really free?

Yes. Every tool here is free to use with no sign-up, no account, and no payment. They are general-information tools, not a substitute for professional advice.

Do I need to sign up to use the tools?

No. The tools work right here in your browser — no account, no name or email needed.

Are the tools medical advice?

No. The tools give general information and ranges, not diagnoses, prescriptions, or personal medical advice. For health concerns, speak to a qualified clinician or dietitian.

Do I need the Lookauramax app to use these tools?

No. The tools work here on the website for everyone. The app adds an AuraScan, a 90-day Aura Cycle, and daily routines if you want to go further.
