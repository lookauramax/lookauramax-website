About Lookauramax

Lookauramax is a private, on-device glow-up app for Android. It gives you an on-device AuraScan, a 90-day Aura Plan, a daily Aura Routine, and a rule-based Aura Coach. Its tagline is "Your glow-up, made real."

What Lookauramax is

  • AuraScan — private on-device analysis. Your photo is analysed on your phone and never uploaded.
  • Aura Score — only actionable traits (skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, photo quality), shown as bands: Needs Work, Good, Great.
  • Aura Plan — journeys in 90-day cycles: 90, 180, 365 days, or ongoing.
  • Aura Routine — small daily tasks, streaks, and at most one reminder a day.
  • Aura Coach — rule-based, practical answers. No medical advice, no extreme diets, no shaming.
  • Aura Preview, Aura Style Lab, Aura Library, Aura Progress, Aura Photo, Aura Badges — preview, style guidance, sourced learning, progress tracking, photo-quality guidance, and badges for consistency.

What Lookauramax is not

  • Not an AI face-rating app. AuraScan is a rule-based estimate, not AI.
  • Not a medical app. It does not diagnose, treat, or promise any physical outcome.
  • Not a photo-upload app. Photos and face analysis stay on your device and are never uploaded or stored in the cloud.
  • Not a weekly-billing trap. Plans are Monthly, 3-Month, 6-Month, and Yearly. No Weekly, no Lifetime, no permanent free plan, no ads.
  • Not a shame machine. It never scores attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, inferred gender, medical conditions, surgery need, comparison with others, or skin lightness.
  • Not a spot-reduction seller. It never claims face exercises, mewing, chewing, or face yoga burn facial fat or give you a jawline.

The honest standard

  • Every price is published on the site before you pay. The price you see is the price in Google Play.
  • Every score explains Why, Action, and confidence. Low confidence means no score — not a low score.
  • Cancel in 2 taps in Google Play. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Reviews on this site come only from real users who gave permission. We never buy reviews, never write our own, and never invent user counts or results.
  • Ages 16+. Under 16 is blocked. Ages 16–17 need parental consent before a scan or paid access.
