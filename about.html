About Lookauramax

Lookauramax is a glow-up app for Android. It gives you an AuraScan, a 90-day Aura Plan, a daily Aura Routine, and an Aura Coach, powered by Aura Intelligence. Its tagline is "Your glow-up, made real."

Illustration — AI-generated image of a fictional person, Day 0 to Day 90. Not a real user or result.

What Lookauramax is

  • AuraScan — analysis from one photo. Your photo is analyzed for your scan.
  • Aura Score — only actionable traits (skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, photo quality), shown as bands: Needs Work, Good, Great.
  • Aura Plan — journeys in Aura Cycles: 90, 180, 365 days, or ongoing.
  • Aura Routine — small daily tasks, Aura Streaks, and at most one reminder a day.
  • Aura Coach — clear, explainable, practical answers. No medical advice, no extreme diets, no shaming.
  • Aura Preview, Aura Style Lab, Aura Library, Aura Progress, Aura Photo, Aura Badges — preview, style guidance, sourced learning, progress tracking, photo-quality guidance, and badges for consistency.

App preview — interface illustration, not a screenshot.

What Lookauramax is not

  • Not an AI face-rating app. AuraScan is an Aura Intelligence estimate, not AI.
  • Not a medical app. It does not diagnose, treat, or promise any physical outcome.
  • Not a weekly-billing trap. Plans are Monthly, 3-Month, 6-Month, and Yearly. No Weekly, no Lifetime, no permanent free plan, no ads.
  • Not a shame machine. It never scores attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, inferred gender, medical conditions, surgery need, comparison with others, or skin lightness.
  • Not a spot-reduction seller. It never claims face exercises, mewing, chewing, or face yoga burn facial fat or give you a jawline.

The honest standard

  • Every price is published on the site before you pay. The price you see is the price in Google Play.
  • Every score explains Why, Action, and confidence. Low confidence means no score — not a low score.
  • Cancel in 2 taps in Google Play. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Reviews on this site come only from real users who gave permission. We never buy reviews, never write our own, and never invent user counts or results.
  • Ages 16+. Under 16 is blocked. Ages 16–17 need parental consent before a scan or paid access.

Lookauramax: pros and cons, honestly

Pros: Aura Intelligence scores, not AI ratings; honest 90-day plans; every price published; cancel in 2 taps; works offline; no ads.

Cons: Android only (no iPhone); joining is required (no guest mode); no permanent free plan; the app is launching after closed testing, so Play Store reviews do not exist yet.

Global availability

Lookauramax is a global platform, not a region-centric one. It launches worldwide on Google Play with local pricing in 10 launch markets: India, the United States, the United Kingdom, Brazil, Indonesia, the Philippines, Mexico, Nigeria, Pakistan, and Bangladesh. The exact price is always shown in Google Play before you pay. The site and guides are in English at launch.

Pricing

US: Monthly $9.99, 3-Month $19.99, 6-Month $29.99, Yearly $49.99. India: Monthly ₹199, 3-Month ₹399, 6-Month ₹599, Yearly ₹999. Monthly, 3-Month, and 6-Month include a 7-day free trial; Yearly includes a 14-day free trial. The 3-Month plan is Best Value (lowest total cost to cover one full Aura Cycle). The Yearly plan is Best Savings Per Month (lowest per-month cost).
