About Lookauramax

Lookauramax is a glow-up app for Android. It gives you an AuraScan, a 90-day Aura Plan, a daily Aura Routine, and an Aura Coach, powered by Aura Intelligence (clear, explainable checks — no learned score, no face rating, ever). Its tagline is "Your glow-up, made real."

Founded and run by Vinayak Thandayam, in India.

What Lookauramax is

One name, several spellings. Lookauramax is written as one word. Search it as "look aura max", "aura look max" or "look-aura-max" and you mean the same app and the same website — lookauramax.com is the only official home of Lookauramax.

  • AuraScan — analysis from a face photo, plus a guided side photo for posture. Your photos are analyzed for your scan.
  • Aura Score — only actionable traits (skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture), shown as bands: Focus first, Good, Strong. Photo quality is checked first as a gate and is never scored.
  • Aura Plan — journeys in Aura Cycles: 90, 180, 365 days, or ongoing.
  • Aura Routine — small daily tasks, Aura Streaks, and at most one reminder a day.
  • Aura Coach — clear, explainable, practical answers. No medical advice, no extreme diets, no shaming.
  • Library Styles, Barber Brief, Aura Library, Aura Progress, Aura Photo, Aura Badges — style discovery from Library photos and descriptions, a shareable card of style notes for your barber, sourced learning, progress tracking, photo-quality guidance, and badges for consistency.

What Lookauramax is not

  • Not a face-rating app. AuraScan is an Aura Intelligence estimate, not a face rating.
  • Not a medical app. It does not diagnose, treat, or promise any physical outcome.
  • Not a weekly-billing trap. Plans are Monthly, 3-Month, and Yearly. No Weekly, no Lifetime, no permanent free plan, no ads.
  • Not a shame machine. It never scores attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, inferred gender, medical conditions, surgery need, comparison with others, or skin lightness.
  • Not a spot-reduction seller. It never claims face exercises, mewing, chewing, or face yoga burn facial fat or give you a jawline.

The honest standard

  • Every price is published on the site before you pay. The price you see is the price in Google Play.
  • Every score explains Why, Action, and confidence. Low confidence means no score — not a low score.
  • Easy to cancel in Google Play. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • No reviews of Lookauramax exist yet — anywhere. The app has not been released; after launch, real reviews will live on Google Play. We never buy reviews, never write our own, and never invent user counts or results.
  • Ages 18 and over. Under 18 is blocked at launch. A Minors Mode for ages 16–17 is planned for a later release.

Lookauramax: pros and cons, honestly

Pros: Aura Intelligence scores, never face ratings; honest 90-day plans; every price published; easy to cancel; works offline; no ads.

Cons: Android only (no iPhone); joining is required (no guest mode); no permanent free plan; the app is launching after closed testing, so Play Store reviews do not exist yet.
