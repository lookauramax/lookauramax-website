FAQ

Comparison questions, answered.

How does Lookauramax compare to Umax?

Side by side: Lookauramax never rates faces and has no weekly plan; Umax is reported to score attractiveness, jawline, and masculinity, and charges about $3.99/week. Umax's own policy says selfies are collected and face scans kept up to 1 year. Read the full Lookauramax vs Umax comparison.

How does Lookauramax compare to LooksMax AI?

Lookauramax never rates faces; LooksMax AI is reported to score attractiveness, masculinity, and jawline, with about $3.99/week on Android. Its own policy (Feb 2024) states data stays on-device. Read the full Lookauramax vs LooksMax AI comparison.

Does Lookauramax rate faces?

Never. AuraScan scores only seven traits you can improve — skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, photo quality — and shows bands (Focus first, Good, Strong), not an attractiveness score.

Does Lookauramax have a weekly plan?

No. Monthly is the shortest plan. Weekly billing is the trap the comparison table calls out: about $3.99/week is about $208/year.

Does Lookauramax share my photos?

No. Your photo is used only for your scan, never shared. Only small data — scores, plan, progress, streaks, settings — syncs to your account.
