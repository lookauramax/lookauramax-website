Trending in Glow-Up Right Now

Seasonal moments coming up

Winter 2026

Cold air and indoor heating dry skin out. The winter move is barrier care: richer moisturiser, lukewarm showers, and sunscreen still every morning — UV damage does not take winter off.

New Year 2027

The New Year is when most people start a routine. Our New Year 2027 hub covers the 90-day plan in full — start a cycle, keep the routine, and check in at Day 30, 60, and 90.

Wedding season

Grooms commonly start 3–6 months ahead of the wedding: a steady routine, good sleep, daily sunscreen, a haircut 7–10 days before, and no new products in the final week.

Valentine's Day

Valentine's prep is a 7-day routine, not a 7-product haul: gentle cleanse, hydrate, one mask mid-week, and no new actives in the last two days.

Summer

Summer means lighter textures, sweat, and more sun. Keep SPF every morning, rinse after heavy sweating, and keep the routine light rather than adding layers.

Rising, not peaked

PDRN / salmon DNA

PDRN is a DNA fragment derived from salmon, named by Vogue and Beauty Independent as a dominant 2026 K-beauty trend. It is used for repair and hydration support. Evidence for topical use is early, and it is not suitable with a fish allergy.

Skin cycling

Skin cycling is a routine pattern that alternates nights: exfoliant night, retinoid night, then recovery nights with gentle hydration. It spaces out strong actives so skin gets rest between them.

7-skin method

The 7-skin method means applying a hydrating toner in up to seven thin layers. It boosts hydration, and it is optional — two or three layers give most of the benefit.

Barrier repair / skinimalism

Barrier repair and skinimalism mean fewer products and a healthier barrier. The trend is a correction to the 10-step era: keep what works, drop what irritates.

Frequently asked questions

What is PDRN?

PDRN is a DNA fragment derived from salmon, used in Korean skincare for repair and hydration support. It moved from clinics to consumer products in 2026. Evidence for topical use is early. It is not suitable with a fish allergy, and it is not vegan.

Is glass skin safe?

The routine itself — cleanse, hydrate, protect — is safe. The risk is stacking many actives at once, which can irritate skin. Keep it to essentials, introduce one product at a time, and stop anything that stings persistently.

What is skin cycling?

A routine pattern that alternates nights: exfoliant night, retinoid night, then recovery nights with gentle hydration. It spaces out strong actives so skin gets rest between them.

What is the 7-skin method?

Applying a hydrating toner in up to seven thin layers, patting each in. It boosts hydration. It is optional — two or three layers give most of the benefit.

When should a groom start skincare before a wedding?

Common advice is 3–6 months ahead for real change, with even one month of steady care making a visible difference. Avoid new products in the final week.
