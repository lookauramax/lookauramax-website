What is a glow-up app?

A glow-up app is an app that helps you improve grooming, habits, and routines with structure. It usually offers a scan or assessment, a plan, daily tasks, and progress tracking. The honest ones explain every score. They never rate attractiveness or promise a result.

Umax — a widely downloaded face-rating app

Does well: a widely downloaded looksmaxing app, with a large community. Quick and easy to use, fast first result, and its viral simplicity introduced many people to structured self-improvement.

Falls short: per third-party reports, it scores attractiveness, jawline, and masculinity — traits you largely cannot change. LooksMax AI — AI analysis + hairstyle simulator

Does well: a polished experience, a hairstyle simulator many users enjoy, progress tracking, and a community feed for peer support. Its detailed trait breakdowns are genuinely informative for some users.

Falls short: per third-party reports, it scores attractiveness, masculinity, and jawline; pricing is reported at about $3.99 per week on Android and $9.99 per week or $29.99 per month on iOS; a short 3-day trial; and a referral system many users report as unreliable. Full results sit behind the subscription even after a free scan, per reviews. Read our full Lookauramax vs LooksMax AI comparison.

Best for: people who want an AI attractiveness score and a hairstyle simulator.

Free face-score websites — fast, but be careful

Do well: free, no download, immediate result. Fine for one moment of curiosity.

Fall short: they usually upload your photo to servers you know nothing about, show ads, and give you a number with no plan attached. There is no routine, no progress tracking, no coach, and no accountability. Before uploading a face photo to any such site, read its privacy policy and avoid sites that ask for unnecessary permissions or accounts. A free number is not worth an unknown photo trail.

Best for: one moment of curiosity — not for real progress.

How to choose

Ask yourself one question: do you want a number or a plan? A number is fast and forgettable. A plan is slower and actually changes things — grooming, skincare, sleep, posture, style — one day at a time. Whichever you pick, check three things before paying: the exact price and billing period in Google Play, where your photos go, and how cancelling works. Those three answers tell you more about an app than any marketing page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best glow-up app in 2026?

It depends on what you want. For a quick face rating, Umax or LooksMax AI will give you one. For a structured 90-day plan with scores for things you can actually improve, and no weekly billing, Lookauramax is built for that. There is no single best app for everyone.

Are free face-score websites safe?

Be cautious. Free face-score websites often upload your photo to unknown servers, show ads, and give a number with no plan attached. Check their privacy policy before uploading a face photo, and avoid sites that ask for unnecessary permissions.

Do glow-up apps actually work?

No app changes your face by itself. What works is consistent action: grooming, skincare, sleep, exercise, posture, and style. A good app gives you a plan and helps you stick to it. An app that only gives you a score does not change anything on its own.

How much should a glow-up app cost?

Watch out for weekly billing: around $3.99 per week adds up to roughly $208 per year. Lookauramax has no weekly plan — in the US, Monthly is $9.99, 3-Month is $19.99, and Yearly is $49.99. Always check the exact price in Google Play before paying.
