Lookauramax Pricing Explained: Which Plan Is Best Value?

By Lookauramax Team · Last updated: 18 Oct 2026 · General information, not medical advice.

Four plans. One full app. No weekly billing, no lifetime plan, no permanent free plan — and two different "best" titles that mean two different things. Here is the whole pricing picture, honestly.

What is Lookauramax pricing?

Lookauramax pricing covers four plans: Monthly, 3-Month, 6-Month, and Yearly. Monthly, 3-Month, and 6-Month include a 7-day free trial. Yearly includes a 14-day free trial. The 3-Month plan is Best Value. The Yearly plan has the lowest cost per month.

Pricing pages are where apps usually start hiding things: a low teaser price, a weekly renewal in small print, a "free" tier that is really a trial in disguise. Lookauramax pricing is built to avoid all of that. Every plan includes the full app. Every plan has a free trial. Prices are stated in full, per plan, before you pay. And the two "best" labels — Best Value and Best Savings Per Month — are defined precisely, so you can pick the one that matches your goal.

What you get before paying

When you join, you get a one-time Join Reward: 1 AuraScan, 1 retake if confidence is low, a 3-day routine, a Preview teaser, and a Library teaser. No card is needed for the reward, and it is available once per account. There is no permanent free plan — the Join Reward is a genuine preview, not a tier that quietly charges you later.

After the Join Reward, you pick a paid plan and start a free trial. You will not be charged during the trial. Google Play collects a payment method at trial start, and a reminder arrives 2 days before the trial ends.

Best Value vs Best Savings Per Month — the honest difference

These two titles sound similar, but they answer different questions. Getting them confused is how people pick the wrong plan, so here is the precise distinction.

  • 3-Month is BEST VALUE because it is the lowest total cost to cover one full Aura Cycle: $19.99 in the US / ₹399 in India total. If your goal is "run one full Aura Cycle and see what changes," 3-Month is the best-value way to do that.
  • Yearly is BEST SAVINGS PER MONTH because it has the lowest cost per month: $4.17/mo in the US / ₹83.25/mo in India. If your goal is "keep going for a full year," Yearly is the lowest cost per month.

One plan holds each title — never both. The badge on the 3-Month card always carries its subline ("One full Aura Cycle"), and the Yearly badge always carries its subline ("Lowest per month"). No plan is ever called "best value" when the claim is really about per-month cost.

Which plan should you pick?

  • Pick Monthly if you want maximum flexibility and the lowest first commitment, and you are happy paying more per month for it. Good for trying the app for one month.
  • Pick 3-Month if you want to cover one full Aura Cycle at the lowest total cost. This is the default recommendation for most people — it matches the app's Aura Cycle exactly.
  • Pick 6-Month if you know you want two full Aura Cycles back to back — for example, skin in the first cycle and style in the second — and want a lower per-month cost than 3-Month.
  • Pick Yearly if you are committed for a full year and want the lowest per-month cost. The 14-day trial gives you the longest test period.

Whatever you pick, the app is identical. No plan gates features by length: unlimited rescans, full Aura Plan and Routine, Aura Coach, full Preview, Style Lab, full Library, Advanced Progress with 30-day tracking, Aura Photo, Badges, and reminders are included in all four.

Trials, renewal, and cancelling

  • Free trial: Monthly, 3-Month, and 6-Month include a 7-day free trial; Yearly includes a 14-day free trial. You will not be charged during the trial.
  • Reminder: a reminder arrives 2 days before the trial ends (Day 5 for 7-day trials, Day 12 for the 14-day trial).
  • Auto-renewal: unless you cancel, the plan auto-renews at the picked plan's price at the end of the trial and then each period.
  • Cancel: 2 taps in Google Play. Cancel during a trial and Pro stays active until the trial ends, with $0 / ₹0 charged. Deleting the app does not cancel — cancel in Google Play.
  • Already used a trial? Trials are one per device per 90 days plus one per account. If you have already used one, the paywall shows a straight "Subscribe" price with no trial — never a trial promise that charges immediately.
  • Refunds: Google Play handles refunds within 48 hours; after that, contact support for a review within 72 hours. See the Refund Policy.

What Lookauramax does not sell

  • No weekly plan. Weekly billing means 52 renewals a year, and an Aura Cycle cannot fit in 7 days. Monthly is the shortest plan.
  • No lifetime plan. Lifetime plans create perverse incentives to stop improving the app. Subscriptions keep the app maintained.
  • No permanent free plan. The Join Reward is one-time and honest about it.
  • No ads. The app is paid for by subscriptions, not by your attention.

Does Lookauramax have a free trial?
