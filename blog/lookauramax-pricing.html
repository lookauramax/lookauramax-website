Three plans. One full app. No weekly billing, no lifetime plan, no permanent free plan — and two different plan titles that mean two different things. Here is the whole pricing picture, honestly.

What is Lookauramax pricing?

Lookauramax pricing covers three plans: Monthly, 3-Month, and Yearly. Every plan starts with the same 7-day starter: ₹7 in India (a small local starter price in other markets), then the plan price. The 3-Month plan is RECOMMENDED · ONE FULL 90-DAY CYCLE. The Yearly plan has the lowest cost per month.

Thinking month to month? The Monthly Plan page puts the Monthly plan and the current month's dated grooming plan side by side.

Pricing pages are where apps usually start hiding things: a low teaser price, a weekly renewal in small print, a "free" tier that is really a trial in disguise. Lookauramax pricing is built to avoid all of that. Every plan includes the full app. Every plan starts with the same 7-day starter week. Prices are stated in full, per plan, before you pay. And the two labels — RECOMMENDED · ONE FULL 90-DAY CYCLE and BEST SAVINGS PER MONTH — are defined precisely, so you can pick the one that matches your goal.

How paying works

There is no permanent free plan. When the app launches, you pick a plan and start with the 7-day starter — ₹7 in India for your first 7 days, then the plan price. Until then, the launch list carries one email on launch day, and that is the whole offer.

You pay the starter price once — ₹7 in India — and the plan price starts only after the starter week, on the date shown before you pay. Google Play collects a payment method at the start, and a reminder arrives 2 days before the starter week ends.

Recommended vs Best Savings Per Month — the honest difference

These two titles sound similar, but they answer different questions. Getting them confused is how people pick the wrong plan, so here is the precise distinction.

  • 3-Month is RECOMMENDED · ONE FULL 90-DAY CYCLE because it is the lowest total cost to cover one full Aura Cycle: $19.99 in the US / ₹399 in India total. If your goal is "run one full Aura Cycle and see what changes," 3-Month is the best-value way to do that.
  • Yearly is BEST SAVINGS PER MONTH because it has the lowest cost per month: $4.16/mo in the US / ₹83.25/mo in India. If your goal is "keep going for a full year," Yearly is the lowest cost per month.

One plan holds each title — never both. The badge on the 3-Month card always carries its subline ("One full Aura Cycle"), and the Yearly badge always carries its subline ("Lowest per month"). No plan is ever called "Recommended" when the claim is really about per-month cost.

Which plan should you pick?

  • Pick Monthly if you want maximum flexibility and the lowest first commitment, and you are happy paying more per month for it. Good for trying the app for one month.
  • Pick 3-Month if you want to cover one full Aura Cycle at the lowest total cost. This is the default recommendation for most people — it matches the app's Aura Cycle exactly.
  • Pick Yearly if you are committed for a full year and want the lowest per-month cost. The starter week is the same 7 days as every plan — the saving comes from the year itself.

Whatever you pick, the app is identical. No plan gates features by length: Aura Check-ins, full Aura Plan and Routine, Aura Coach and Library, Aura Progress — your trend across check-ins, Aura Photo, Badges, and reminders are included in all three.

The starter week, renewal, and cancelling

  • Starter week: every plan starts with 7 days for ₹7 in India (a small local starter price in other markets), then the plan price. The starter week includes the full report and the first 7 days of your routine.
  • Reminder: a reminder arrives 2 days before the starter week ends (Day 5).
  • Auto-renewal: unless you cancel, the plan auto-renews at the picked plan's price at the end of the starter week and then each period.
  • Cancel: easily in Google Play. Cancel during the starter week and your plan stays active until the week ends, and the plan price is never charged. Deleting the app does not cancel — cancel in Google Play.
  • Already used your starter? The starter price is a one-time welcome for new subscribers. If you have already had it, the paywall shows a straight "Subscribe" price — the full plan price, stated openly.
  • Refunds: Google Play handles refunds within 48 hours; after that, contact support for a review within 72 hours. See the Refund Policy.

What Lookauramax does not sell

  • No weekly plan. Weekly billing means 52 renewals a year, and an Aura Cycle cannot fit in 7 days. Monthly is the shortest plan.
  • No lifetime plan. Lifetime plans create perverse incentives to stop improving the app. Subscriptions keep the app maintained.
  • No permanent free plan. Every plan starts with the 7-day starter for ₹7 in India.
  • No ads. The app is paid for by subscriptions, not by your attention.
