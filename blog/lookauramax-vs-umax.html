What each app measures — the biggest difference

Umax, according to third-party reports and reviews, rates your features on a numerical scale — jawline, masculinity, skin quality, cheekbones, and an overall score. That is the classic face-rating model: a number for things you largely cannot change.

Lookauramax deliberately does not do that. AuraScan is a rule-based estimate — not AI — and it scores only actionable traits: skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, and photo quality. It never scores attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, inferred gender, or medical conditions. Every result explains why, what to do, and how confident the estimate is. Low confidence means no score at all.

Why does this matter? A score for your jawline cannot tell you what to do on Monday morning. A score for your skin can — cleanse, moisturise, sunscreen — and you can see it improve. That is the whole design philosophy: measure what you can change, and help you change it.

Privacy: where your photos go

This is the difference we are proudest of. With Lookauramax, your photos and facial analysis never leave your device. They are never uploaded, never stored in the cloud, never used to train anything. Only small data — scores, plan, progress, streaks, settings — syncs to your account.

Umax, per third-party reports, uploads photos to servers for analysis. That is the standard approach for most face-rating apps, and it is a real privacy trade-off: your face photo is among the most sensitive data you own.

Fairness matters here. Umax is the most downloaded looksmaxing app, with millions of installs and a large community.
