What each app measures — the biggest difference

Umax, according to third-party reports and reviews, rates your features on a numerical scale — jawline, masculinity, skin quality, cheekbones, and an overall score. That is the classic face-rating model: a number for things you largely cannot change.

Lookauramax deliberately does not do that. AuraScan is an Aura Intelligence estimate — not AI — and it scores only actionable traits: skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, and photo quality. It never scores attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, inferred gender, or medical conditions. Every result explains why, what to do, and how confident the estimate is. Low confidence means no score at all.

Why does this matter? A score for your jawline cannot tell you what to do on Monday morning. A score for your skin can — cleanse, moisturise, sunscreen — and you can see it improve. That is the whole design philosophy: measure what you can change, and help you change it.

Pricing: weekly billing adds up

Umax is reported to charge around $3.99 per week in-app. Weekly billing looks small, but it compounds: $3.99 × 52 weeks is roughly $208 per year, and weekly subscriptions are the easiest to forget about.

Lookauramax has no weekly plan — deliberately. In the US: Monthly $9.99, 3-Month $19.99, 6-Month $29.99, Yearly $49.99. In India: Monthly ₹199, 3-Month ₹399, 6-Month ₹599, Yearly ₹999. Monthly, 3-Month, and 6-Month include a 7-day free trial; Yearly includes a 14-day trial. The 3-Month plan is Best Value (lowest total cost to cover one full Aura Cycle); Yearly is Best Savings Per Month (lowest per-month cost). The exact price in your country is always shown in Google Play before you pay.

Privacy: where your photos go

How Lookauramax handles your photo and data is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Umax’s privacy policy lists selfies and biometric data as collected, and says face scans are kept for up to one year. Keeping a face scan for a year is a real privacy trade-off: your face photo is among the most sensitive data you own.

What Umax does well

Fairness matters here. Umax is the most downloaded looksmaxing app, with millions of installs and a large community. It is quick and easy to use, it gives a fast first result, and its viral simplicity introduced millions of people to the idea of structured self-improvement. If you want a quick one-time face rating, it delivers that.

Where it falls short, in our honest view: scores for unchangeable traits, weekly billing that adds up, face scans kept up to a year, per its privacy policy, and many features behind paywalls. Reviewers have also reported that it charges separately for add-ons on top of a subscription.

Our honest cons

We will not pretend we have no weaknesses. Lookauramax is launching after closed testing — the app is not on Google Play yet, so there are no real user reviews yet. Umax has years of history and millions of downloads; we have to earn that trust from zero. What we can offer instead is a cleaner deal: honest scoring, no weekly billing trap, and a plan structure built around 90-day progress rather than one-off ratings.
