FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Lookauramax?

Lookauramax is a private glow-up app for Android. It gives you an on-device AuraScan, a 90-day Aura Plan, a daily Aura Routine, and a rule-based Aura Coach. Your photos never leave your phone.

Is Lookauramax free?

There is no permanent free plan. When you join, you get a one-time Join Reward: 1 AuraScan, 1 retake if confidence is low, a 3-day routine, and a Preview teaser — no card needed. After that, you pick a paid plan and start a free trial.

Does Lookauramax upload my photos?

No. Photos and face analysis stay on your device and are never uploaded or stored in the cloud. Only small data — scores, plan, progress, streaks, settings — syncs to your account.

Does Lookauramax use AI to rate my face?

No. AuraScan is a rule-based estimate, not AI. It scores only actionable traits and shows bands (Needs Work, Good, Great), not an attractiveness score.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel in 2 taps in Google Play before the trial ends and you pay nothing. If you cancel during a trial, Pro stays active until the trial ends. Deleting the app does not cancel — cancel in Google Play.

Which plan is best value?

The 3-Month plan is Best Value — the lowest total cost to complete one full 90-day transformation: ₹399 in India / $19.99 in the US. The Yearly plan has the lowest cost per month.

Who can use Lookauramax?

Ages 16 and over. Under 16 is blocked. Ages 16–17 need parental consent before a scan or paid access.

When does the app launch?

The Lookauramax website is live now. The app launches on Google Play after closed testing is complete.
