Height: The Honest Truth (and What You Can Control)

By Lookauramax Team · Published 17 Oct 2026 · General information, not medical advice.

Can you grow taller as an adult? No. Can you look taller, stand taller, and carry yourself better? Yes — and that is where the real change is.

Height is one of the most searched and most scammed topics in looksmaxing. The internet is full of "grow 6 inches in 30 days" videos. Here is the truth, plainly: your adult height is set by genetics and by the growth plates at the ends of your long bones. Once those plates close — usually in the late teens for most people — no pill, powder, exercise, stretch, or device makes your bones longer.

Why nothing grows adults taller

Height comes from bone length. Long bones grow at growth plates (epiphyseal plates) during childhood and adolescence. When puberty ends, those plates fuse and close. After that, the bones cannot lengthen. This is basic human biology, not a matter of effort or belief. Anyone selling a product that claims to reopen growth plates in adults is selling a scam.

For teenagers who are still growing, the things that genuinely matter are the boring ones: enough sleep, balanced nutrition, regular activity, and treating any medical condition. No supplement makes a growing teenager taller than their genetic range — but poor nutrition and chronic sleep deprivation can hold growth back. If a teenager is far shorter than expected for their family, a doctor can check for treatable causes. That is a medical conversation, not a YouTube routine.

The scams to avoid

  • Height-increase pills and powders: do not work. Some contain unregulated hormones or stimulants and can be unsafe. Do not buy them.
  • "Grow taller" exercises and stretches: hanging, cobra stretches, and inversion tables do not lengthen bones. They may decompress the spine by a millimetre or two for a short while — you are not growing.
  • Height-increase shoes with hidden lifts marketed as growth: lifts change how tall you look in shoes, not your body. As a style choice they are fine; as a growth claim they are dishonest.
  • Limb-lengthening surgery: a serious, painful, expensive orthopaedic procedure with real risks, done for medical reasons. It is not a glow-up shortcut, and it should never be marketed as one.

What you CAN control

Your height is fixed. Your apparent height is not. Most people lose 2–5 cm of visible height to slouching, poor posture, and clothes that break their silhouette. Fixing those things can make a visible difference:

  • Posture: standing tall with shoulders relaxed and chin level — not jutting forward — can add centimetres to how tall you look. Our posture guide explains the daily reset.
  • Fit and style: well-fitted clothes, monochrome or tonal outfits, and clean lines from head to toe. Our look-taller guide covers this in full.
  • Strength and body composition: a stronger, leaner frame looks taller and more confident than a slouched one at the same height. See our muscle-building guide.
  • Photos: a low camera angle and good posture change perceived height dramatically. See our scan photo tips.
  • Confidence: how you enter a room matters more than a centimetre. Eye contact, clear speech, and relaxed posture read as presence.

Why this matters

Height insecurity is real, and the scam industry feeds on it. The honest answer is freeing: stop spending money and hope on growing taller, and put that energy into the things that actually change how you look and feel — posture, fitness, grooming, style, and confidence. Those are controllable, and they compound.

Frequently asked questions

Can adults grow taller?

No. Once the growth plates close after puberty, no exercise, supplement, or device increases adult height. Claims otherwise are scams.

Do height-increase pills or supplements work?

No. Height-increase pills, powders, and devices do not grow adults taller. Some are unsafe. Do not buy them.

Can stretching or hanging make you taller?

No. Stretching and hanging may decompress the spine temporarily by millimetres, but they do not increase bone length or lasting height.

What can make you look taller?

Good posture, well-fitted clothes, monochrome outfits, and clean footwear lines can make you look visibly taller without changing your height.
