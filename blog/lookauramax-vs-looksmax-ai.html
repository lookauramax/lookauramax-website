What each app measures — the biggest difference

LooksMax AI, according to third-party reports and reviews, analyses selfies and returns scores for attractiveness, masculinity, jawline, hairline, and eyes, plus a hairstyle simulator in its Pro tier. Its core product is a judgement about your face.

Lookauramax deliberately does not do that. AuraScan is a rule-based estimate — not AI — and it scores only actionable traits: skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, and photo quality. It never scores attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, inferred gender, or medical conditions. Every result explains why, what to do, and how confident the estimate is. Low confidence means no score at all.

The difference is practical. A masculinity score cannot tell you what to do on Monday morning. A skin score can — cleanse, moisturise, sunscreen — and you can watch it improve. That is the whole design philosophy: measure what you can change, and help you change it.

Pricing: weekly billing adds up

Lookauramax has no weekly plan — deliberately. In the US: Monthly $9.99, 3-Month $19.99, 6-Month $29.99, Yearly $49.99. In India: Monthly ₹199, 3-Month ₹399, 6-Month ₹599, Yearly ₹999. Monthly, 3-Month, and 6-Month include a 7-day free trial; Yearly includes a 14-day trial. The 3-Month plan is Best Value (lowest total cost to complete one 90-day transformation); Yearly is Best Savings Per Month (lowest per-month cost). The exact price in your country is always shown in Google Play before you pay.

Privacy: where your photos go

With Lookauramax, your photos and facial analysis never leave your device. They are never uploaded, never stored in the cloud, never used to train anything. Only small data — scores, plan, progress, streaks, settings — syncs to your account.

LooksMax AI, per third-party reports, uploads selfies to servers for AI analysis. Some reports state images are deleted after processing; even so, uploading a face photo is a real privacy trade-off, because your face photo is among the most sensitive data you own.

What LooksMax AI does well

Fairness matters here. LooksMax AI has a polished experience, a hairstyle simulator that many users enjoy, progress tracking, and a community feed for people who want peer support. It helped popularise AI face analysis, and its detailed trait breakdowns are genuinely informative for some users.

Where it falls short, in our honest view: scores for unchangeable traits, weekly billing that adds up, a short 3-day trial, photos leaving your device, and a referral system that many users report as unreliable. Reviewers have also reported that full results sit behind the subscription even after a free scan.

What we offer instead is a cleaner deal: honest scoring, on-device privacy, no weekly billing trap, and a plan structure built around 90-day progress rather than one-off ratings.
