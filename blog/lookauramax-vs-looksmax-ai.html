What each app measures — the biggest difference

LooksMax AI, according to third-party reports and reviews, analyses selfies and returns scores for attractiveness, masculinity, jawline, hairline, and eyes, plus a hairstyle simulator in its Pro tier. Its core product is a judgement about your face.

Lookauramax deliberately does not do that. AuraScan is an Aura Intelligence estimate — not AI — and it scores only actionable traits: skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, and photo quality. It never scores attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, inferred gender, or medical conditions. Every result explains why, what to do, and how confident the estimate is. Low confidence means no score at all.

The difference is practical. A masculinity score cannot tell you what to do on Monday morning. A skin score can — cleanse, moisturise, sunscreen — and you can watch it improve. That is the whole design philosophy: measure what you can change, and help you change it.

Pricing: weekly billing adds up

LooksMax AI is reported at about $3.99 per week on Android, and $9.99 per week or $29.99 per month on iOS. A 3-day trial is reported. Cancel via device subscription settings.

Lookauramax has no weekly plan — deliberately. In the US: Monthly $9.99, 3-Month $19.99, Yearly $49.99. In India: Monthly ₹199, 3-Month ₹399, Yearly ₹999. Every plan starts with 7 days for ₹7 in India (a small local starter price in other markets), then the plan price. The 3-Month plan is our Recommended plan (lowest total cost to cover one full Aura Cycle); Yearly is Best Savings Per Month (lowest per-month cost). The exact price in your country is always shown in Google Play before you pay.

Privacy: where your photos go

How Lookauramax handles your photo and data is explained in the Privacy Policy.

LooksMax AI states in its privacy policy (Feb 2024) that it does not collect personal data and that data stays on-device. We have not independently verified how analysis is performed. Either way, a face photo is among the most sensitive data you own, so how any app handles it matters.

What LooksMax AI does well

Fairness matters here. LooksMax AI has a polished experience, a hairstyle simulator that many users enjoy, progress tracking, and a community feed for people who want peer support. It helped popularise AI face analysis, and its detailed trait breakdowns are genuinely informative for some users.

Where it falls short, in our honest view: scores for unchangeable traits, weekly billing that adds up, a short 3-day trial, and a referral system that many users report as unreliable. Reviewers have also reported that full results sit behind the subscription even after a free scan.

Our honest cons

We will not pretend we have no weaknesses. Lookauramax is launching after closed testing — the app is not on Google Play yet, so there are no real user reviews yet. LooksMax AI has years of history; we have to earn that trust from zero. We also do not offer a hairstyle simulator or a community feed at launch. What we offer instead is a cleaner deal: honest scoring, no weekly billing trap, and a plan structure built around 90-day progress rather than one-off ratings.
