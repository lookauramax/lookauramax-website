Lookauramax is for adults aged 18 and over

Lookauramax is currently available to adults aged 18 and over. Sign-up is blocked for anyone under 18, and no account can be created without passing the age gate.

A Minors Mode for ages 16–17, with parental consent, is planned for a later release and is not available yet. Until it launches, parental consent cannot be requested, given, or confirmed — including on this page.

If you received an email asking you to confirm parental consent for Lookauramax, no action is needed — nothing will be confirmed.

Questions? Contact us at support@lookauramax.com.
