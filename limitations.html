What Lookauramax can — and cannot — do

Lookauramax is a glow-up companion: it reads seven actionable signals from one photo, explains every result, and turns it into a 90-day plan. This page states plainly where its abilities end.

What it helps with

  • Reading skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, and posture signals from a photo — each with a Why, an Action, and a confidence level.
  • Checking photo quality before scoring, so a bad photo never becomes an unfair result.
  • Building a 90-day Aura Plan and a daily Aura Routine that adapt as you check in.
  • Tracking progress over time with Aura Check-ins, in a private progress timeline.
  • Honest pricing: every plan shown openly, a 7-day starter for ₹7 on every plan, easy to cancel in Google Play.

How to read a result

Each signal gets a band — Focus first, Good, or Strong. A band is guidance for that one signal, not a rating of you, and never an attractiveness score. The app shows no overall number, because a single number would claim a precision the checks do not have.

Photo Quality is a gate, not a score. It is checked before scoring: light, sharpness, and framing. A photo that cannot be read fairly asks for a retake. A signal that still cannot be read shows Not assessed, with the fix path — and you still get a baseline routine. An unreadable signal is never given a guessed score.

What it cannot do

  • Diagnose or treat any medical, skin, dental, or mental-health condition.
  • Score attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, or anything you cannot act on.
  • Predict or guarantee an outcome. Plans run in 90-day cycles; change depends on consistency, and flat progress at a check-in is information, not failure.
  • Work from a photo that fails the quality gate — it will ask for a better one instead.
  • Replace a dermatologist, doctor, dietitian, or any qualified professional.

If a claim cannot be verified, it does not ship. That rule built this page.
