FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Lookauramax?

Lookauramax is a private glow-up app for Android. It gives you an on-device AuraScan, a 90-day Aura Plan, a daily Aura Routine, and a rule-based Aura Coach. Your photos never leave your phone.

Is Lookauramax free?

There is no permanent free plan. When you join, you get a one-time Join Reward: 1 AuraScan, 1 retake if confidence is low, a 3-day routine, and a Preview teaser — no card needed. After that, you pick a paid plan and start a free trial.

Does Lookauramax upload my photos?

No. Photos and face analysis stay on your device and are never uploaded or stored in the cloud. Only small data — scores, plan, progress, streaks, settings — syncs to your account.

Does Lookauramax use AI to rate my face?

No. AuraScan is a rule-based estimate, not AI. It scores only actionable traits — skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, and photo quality — and shows bands (Needs Work, Good, Great), not an attractiveness score.

What does Lookauramax never score?

Lookauramax never scores attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, race, inferred gender, medical conditions, surgery need, comparison with others, or skin lightness.

How much does Lookauramax cost?

In India: Monthly ₹199, 3-Month ₹399, 6-Month ₹599, Yearly ₹999. In the US: Monthly $9.99, 3-Month $19.99, 6-Month $29.99, Yearly $49.99. Monthly, 3-Month and 6-Month include a 7-day free trial; Yearly includes a 14-day free trial. Prices in other countries vary; the exact price is shown in Google Play before you pay.

Which Lookauramax plan is best value?

The 3-Month plan is Best Value because it is the lowest total cost to complete one full 90-day transformation: ₹399 in India / $19.99 in the US. The Yearly plan has the lowest cost per month.

Can I cancel Lookauramax?

Yes. Cancel in 2 taps in Google Play before the trial ends and you pay nothing. If you cancel during a trial, Pro stays active until the trial ends. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription — cancel in Google Play.

Who can use Lookauramax?

Lookauramax is for ages 16 and over. Users under 16 are blocked. Ages 16–17 need parental consent before a scan or paid access.

Does Lookauramax promise a jawline or face-fat loss?

No. Spot reduction is a myth. Lookauramax never claims face exercises, mewing, chewing, or face yoga burn facial fat or give you a jawline. Jawline appearance help is limited to grooming, posture, lighting, and photo angle.

Does Lookauramax work offline?

Yes. The core app — scan, plan, routine, coach — works offline. You need internet for joining, syncing small data, and billing.

Is Lookauramax on iPhone?

No. Lookauramax launches on Android (Google Play) only.
