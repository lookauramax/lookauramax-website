How AuraScan Works

From one photo to a plan.

Three steps. No rating of your face, no score you cannot act on — a read, an explanation, and a day-by-day routine.

01

Scan

Take one well-lit photo. AuraScan checks photo quality first — light, sharpness, framing — then reads the seven signals. A photo that cannot be read fairly asks for a retake instead of a score.

02

Understand

Every signal comes back as a band — Focus first, Good, or Strong — with its Why and its Action: what the read means, and what to do about it. Low confidence means no score, never a low score.

03

Do today

Your Aura Plan turns the findings into a daily routine inside a 90-day Aura Cycle, with an Aura Check-in at Day 30, 60, and 90 to measure real change.

What AuraScan Measures

Seven signals. Only what you can improve.

AuraScan reads seven traits from one photo — and nothing else. Each one comes with a band, a Why, an Action, and a confidence level. Tap a signal to see how it is read.

Clarity, evenness, and texture are visible in most well-lit photos — and they respond to a steady routine.

The action

Progress is a routine kept, not a result promised. Your plan runs in 90-day Aura Cycles, with an Aura Check-in at Day 30, 60, and 90 to re-read your signals under the same conditions.

Day 1

Your AuraScan sets the baseline — seven bands, each with its Why and Action.

Day 30

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Lookauramax?

Lookauramax is a glow-up app for Android that gives you an AuraScan, a 90-day Aura Plan, a daily Aura Routine, and an Aura Coach, powered by Aura Intelligence (clear, explainable checks — not AI).

Is Look Aura Max the same as Lookauramax?

Yes. Lookauramax is one word, but it is often searched with spaces or hyphens — "look aura max", "aura look max", "look-aura-max". Every spacing refers to the same official app and website. Our only website is lookauramax.com; a similar name anywhere else is not us.

Is Lookauramax free?

There is no permanent free plan. When the app launches, you pick a paid plan and start with the 7-day starter — ₹7 in India for your first 7 days, then the plan price. Join the launch list for one email on launch day.

Does Lookauramax upload my photos?

No. Your photo is used only for your scan, never shared. Only small data — scores, plan, progress, streaks, settings — syncs to your account.

Does Lookauramax use AI to rate my face?

No. AuraScan is an Aura Intelligence estimate, not AI. It scores only actionable traits and shows bands (Focus first, Good, Strong), not an attractiveness score.

Can I cancel?

Yes — easy to cancel in Google Play. Cancel during the starter week and the plan price is never charged; Pro stays active until the starter week ends. Deleting the app does not cancel — cancel in Google Play.

Which plan is best value?

The 3-Month plan is our Recommended plan — the lowest total cost to cover one full Aura Cycle: ₹399 in India / $19.99 in the US. The Yearly plan has the lowest cost per month.

Who can use Lookauramax?

Ages 18 and over. Under 18 is blocked at launch. A Minors Mode for ages 16–17 is planned for a later release.

When does the app launch?

The Lookauramax website is live now. The app launches on Google Play after closed testing is complete.

Why is Lookauramax the best glow-up app?

We built it to be the best by being the most honest: scores only for traits you can improve, every score with Why + Action, honest pricing shown openly, no weekly billing trap, no face ratings, easy to cancel — and our own cons published in our comparisons. We claim no ranking, award, or user count we cannot prove.

What is an Aura Score?

Your Aura Score is a band — Focus first, Good, or Strong — for one of the seven Aura Signals, or for your overall result. Every band comes with Why (what the scan measured) and Action (what to do next), plus a confidence level. The app never shows a number: bands keep the result honest and actionable.

What are Aura Signals?

The seven traits AuraScan measures: skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, and photo quality. Only traits you can act on are scored — never attractiveness, bone structure, jaw shape, age, or race.

What is Aura Intelligence?

The name for Lookauramax’s scoring engine. It uses clear, explainable checks on your photo — not AI, not machine learning. Every score shows its Why and Action, so you can see exactly what was measured and what to do about it.

Is looksmaxing safe?

The habits Lookauramax teaches — skincare, grooming, posture, sleep, style, fitness — are safe everyday habits. What is not safe: extreme diets, unapproved substances, DIY procedures, or chasing a look at the cost of your health. Lookauramax never recommends those, and it never scores anything that would push you toward them.

What makes Lookauramax trustworthy?

It shows its work: every score carries Why + Action + confidence, pricing is shown openly before you pay, comparisons publish our own cons, and it never claims a ranking, award, or user count it cannot prove. If a claim cannot be verified, it does not ship.
