What AuraScan measures

Seven signals. Only what you can improve.

AuraScan reads seven traits from one photo — and nothing else. Each one comes with a band, a Why, an Action, and a confidence level. Low confidence means no score.

01

Skin

Reads: clarity, evenness, texture signals.

Never: skin tone or lightness.

02

Hair

Reads: fullness, hairline shape, grooming.

Never: promises about regrowth.

03

Brows

Reads: shape, grooming, fullness.

Never: bone structure.

04

Beard

Reads: density, neckline, edges.

Never: scored unless you opt in.

05

Smile

Reads: teeth visibility, lip care, expression.

Never: dental diagnosis.

06

Posture

Reads: head position, shoulder line, stance.

Never: medical diagnosis.

07

Photo Quality

Reads: light, sharpness, framing.

Never: an unfair score from a bad photo.

We built Lookauramax to be the best glow-up app — not by promising more, but by refusing the tricks the rest of the market runs on. These are not marketing lines. They are product rules, and you can check every one of them before you pay.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Lookauramax?

Lookauramax is a glow-up app for Android that gives you an AuraScan, a 90-day Aura Plan, a daily Aura Routine, and an Aura Coach, powered by Aura Intelligence.

Is Lookauramax free?

There is no permanent free plan. When you join, you get a one-time Join Reward: 1 AuraScan, 1 retake if confidence is low, a 3-day routine, a Preview teaser, and a Library teaser — no card needed. After that, you pick a paid plan and start a free trial.

Does Lookauramax upload my photos?

No. Your photo is used only for your scan, never shared. Only small data — scores, plan, progress, streaks, settings — syncs to your account.

Does Lookauramax use AI to rate my face?

No. AuraScan is an Aura Intelligence estimate, not AI. It scores only actionable traits and shows bands (Needs Work, Good, Great), not an attractiveness score.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel in 2 taps in Google Play before the trial ends and you pay nothing. If you cancel during a trial, Pro stays active until the trial ends. Deleting the app does not cancel — cancel in Google Play.

Which plan is best value?

The 3-Month plan is Best Value — the lowest total cost to cover one full Aura Cycle: ₹399 in India / $19.99 in the US. The Yearly plan has the lowest cost per month.

Who can use Lookauramax?

Ages 16 and over. Under 16 is blocked. Ages 16–17 need parental consent before a scan or paid access.

When does the app launch?

The Lookauramax website is live now. The app launches on Google Play after closed testing is complete.

Why is Lookauramax the best glow-up app?

We built it to be the best by being the most honest: scores only for traits you can improve, every score with Why + Action, honest pricing shown openly, no weekly billing trap, no face ratings ever, cancel in 2 taps — and our own cons published in our comparisons.
