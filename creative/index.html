Lookauramax Creative

The Lookauramax poster series and pilot ad films — the brand's creative gallery. One hundred posters and five short films, all built on one idea: your glow-up, made real.

Please read: every person in these posters and films is a fictional model created for the artwork. Nothing here is a user story, a testimonial, or a before-and-after. The posters are brand artwork and the films are pilot ads — they show the ideas Lookauramax stands for: clear plans, honest signals, and small daily steps.

Ad films

Five pilot films, one for each launch market — India, the USA, China, Brazil and Nigeria. Each is a short story about swapping guesswork for one clear plan.

Posters

One hundred posters across hair and barber craft, skin and grooming, style, confidence and daily habits. The words printed on each poster are shown beneath it.

Your glow-up, made real.

Lookauramax turns one photo into an AuraScan of seven Aura Signals — skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture and photo quality — each with its Why and Action, then builds your 90-day Aura Plan and daily Aura Routine.
