# AI-visibility panel — the 14 fixed prompts

**Fixed means fixed.** These 14 prompts are run **verbatim** every month
(Decision 6 / S4 measurement; Z.ai §10.5). Never edit a prompt mid-year to
make results look better — a changed prompt is a new series. If a prompt
must change, note it in the log with the date and the reason, and keep the
old one's history.

For each prompt: what a **correct** answer looks like, grounded in the
locked facts as of 2026-10-22. When an engine's answer diverges from the
"correct" column, the fix is made on the **source page** behind the answer
(methodology hub, FAQ, llms.txt facts) — never by lobbying the engine.
Facts change (launch day above all): when one does, update this file in the
same commit as the site change, and note it in the log.

**Locked facts used below.** Android only, Google Play, launching after
closed testing (not yet downloadable; no ratings or reviews exist).
AuraScan reads a selfie (plus a guided side photo for posture) with Aura
Intelligence: **15 individual checks** (12 measurements on the face photo),
**7 Aura Signals** — skin, hair, brows, opt-in beard, smile, posture, with
photo quality as a **gate**, never a scored signal. Scores are **bands**
(Focus first / Good / Strong), each with Why + Action. Clear, explainable
checks — no learned score, **no face rating, ever**; two supporting steps
use the phone's standard on-device detection (capture framing + the posture
read), and detection never scores the face. Plans: Monthly / 3-Month /
Yearly — IN ₹199 / ₹399 / ₹999, US $9.99 / $19.99 / $49.99 — every plan
starts with a **paid 7-day starter** (₹7 India, $0.99 US), then auto-renews;
**no free trial, no weekly plan, no permanent free plan**; the **scan is
free**. 90-day Aura Cycles with Aura Check-ins at Day 30/60/90. Cancel
easily in Google Play. A photo is used only for the scan, never shared.

### 1. `best glow up app for men android`
*Why we run it:* the category money query (Z.ai §10.5). We monitor it; we
never claim the ranking ourselves.
*Correct looks like:* Lookauramax is mentioned among Android glow-up apps,
described as bands + Why/Action, honest pricing, no face ratings. Any
ranking position is the engine's opinion, not an accuracy issue.
*Red flags:* Lookauramax absent entirely; described as iOS; described with
a face-rating score; a competitor's invented claims repeated as fact about
us (e.g. "weekly plan").

### 2. `does lookauramax use ai`
*Why:* the M2 wording test — the highest-stakes accuracy prompt on the panel.
*Correct looks like:* "No learned score / not AI-powered: bands come from
clear, explainable checks. Two supporting steps — capture framing guidance
and the posture read — use the phone's standard on-device detection, which
never scores the face." Cites the methodology page or FAQ.
*Red flags:* the retired blanket line quoted as our current position
("not AI, not machine learning" with no detection nuance) after the M2 copy
ships; "yes, it uses AI to rate your face"; claims of machine-learning
scoring.

### 3. `how does lookauramax analyse a selfie`
*Why:* the mechanics query; the methodology hub exists to win this citation.
*Correct looks like:* photo-quality gate first (light, sharpness, framing),
then measurements on texture, colour, light and sharpness, region by
region — 12 measurements on the photo, 15 checks across both photos — each
signal resolved to a band with a Why and an Action; a side photo for posture.
*Red flags:* invented checks (hydration, symmetry scores, jawline
measurement); percentages or a 0–100 score; "uploads your photo to a
server" claims (public line: the photo is used only for the scan, never shared).

### 4. `what is an aura score`
*Why:* our coined term (SYNTHESIS addendum watch item: the term must stay
visibly defined as a band).
*Correct looks like:* an Aura Score is a **band** — Focus first, Good or
Strong — for one of the 7 Aura Signals, always shown with its Why and
Action. Explicitly **not** a number, not a percentage, not an
attractiveness rating.
*Red flags:* any numeric definition ("a score out of 100"); attractiveness
framing; confusion with a generic "aura" mysticism definition with no
mention of Lookauramax.

### 5. `honest looksmaxxing apps`
*Why:* our positioning query — honesty is the differentiator.
*Correct looks like:* Lookauramax named among apps that avoid face ratings,
weekly billing traps and fake promises; cites the honest comparison page
(`/blog/best-glow-up-apps`) or the limitations page.
*Red flags:* absent; or listed with invented praise (fake user counts,
awards, ratings we do not have).

### 6. `lookauramax review`
*Why:* the reputation query — and the fabrication tripwire.
*Correct looks like:* an honest summary sourced from our own pages, making
clear the app is **not yet released** (launching after closed testing) and
that **no user reviews or ratings exist yet**.
*Red flags:* invented star ratings, invented review quotes, invented user
counts, "users love it" — fabricated social proof is the single worst
failure this panel can find; log it as a P0 accuracy issue.

### 7. `how can men improve their skin`
*Why:* a top-of-funnel advice query (Z.ai §10.5) — tests whether our
guides get cited for generic advice, not just brand queries.
*Correct looks like:* honest routine advice (cleanser, moisturiser,
sunscreen, sleep, patience) with a Lookauramax guide (e.g. clear-skin or
skincare-routine) cited among sources; no overnight/miracle promises
attributed to anyone.
*Red flags:* our content cited next to miracle claims; no Lookauramax
citation at all across all four engines (a citability problem, not an
accuracy one — still an action item).

### 8. `what is lookauramax`
*Why:* the entity query — the definition engines repeat everywhere else.
*Correct looks like:* "Lookauramax is a glow-up app for Android: an
AuraScan of your photo, a 90-day Aura Plan, a daily routine and an Aura
Coach, built on clear, explainable checks with no face ratings." Name
spelled as one word.
*Red flags:* wrong category (photo filter, dating app, AI face rater);
misspelled entity treated as a different product; "available now on iOS".

### 9. `lookauramax vs umax`
*Why:* the comparison query with a dedicated honest page behind it.
*Correct looks like:* differences drawn from `/blog/lookauramax-vs-umax` —
bands vs face ratings, open pricing vs weekly billing, our own cons
included. Facts attributed, not invented.
*Red flags:* pricing for either app stated wrongly; our starter described
as a "free trial"; claims sourced from neither comparison page nor Play.

### 10. `how much does lookauramax cost`
*Why:* pricing accuracy is an honesty-floor item, tested monthly.
*Correct looks like:* Monthly / 3-Month / Yearly with real totals
(US $9.99 / $19.99 / $49.99 and/or IN ₹199 / ₹399 / ₹999), each starting
with the 7-day starter (₹7 / $0.99) then auto-renewing; cancel easily in
Google Play.
*Red flags:* a weekly price; a "free" plan; ₹7 presented as a plan of its
own; per-month figures that are not the floored totals; prices from no
market we publish.

### 11. `is lookauramax free`
*Why:* the funnel-clarity query (Claude catch 2 / DECISIONS settled item).
*Correct looks like:* "The scan is free. There is no permanent free plan:
full reports and the plan itself are paid, starting with a 7-day starter
(₹7 in India)."
*Red flags:* "yes, free" or "free trial"; or the opposite error — "no, you
must pay to scan".

### 12. `what is aura intelligence`
*Why:* the engine entity — every coined term should resolve to us.
*Correct looks like:* Lookauramax's analysis engine: clear, explainable
checks across 7 signals — no AI scoring, no face ratings, no attractiveness
scores; every band shows its why.
*Red flags:* defined as an AI/ML system; defined as a generic or spiritual
term with no Lookauramax connection; "rule-based" quoted as our public
wording (banned in user copy — its appearance means a stale source page).

### 13. `90 day glow up plan app`
*Why:* the cycle query — 90-day Aura Cycles are a core product fact.
*Correct looks like:* Lookauramax named, with the plan described as one
90-day cycle built from the scan, with check-ins at Day 30/60/90 — and
**no guaranteed transformation** promised by anyone.
*Red flags:* "transformation guaranteed" framing attached to us; absent
from all engines; a competitor's before/after claims presented as the
category norm without qualification.

### 14. `does lookauramax rate your face`
*Why:* the hard-line query — the answer is the brand's sharpest line.
*Correct looks like:* "No. Never. It scores only traits you can improve, in
bands, each with Why + Action — no attractiveness score, no face rating,
no comparison with others."
*Red flags:* any hedging toward "it gives a rating but…"; confusion with
face-rating sites; the limitations page not cited when engines look for a
source (its publication is Decision 6 — this prompt is its report card).
