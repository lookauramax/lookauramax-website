Common misconceptions

  • More products do not mean faster results. Add one product at a time, with weeks between additions.
  • Sunscreen protects skin; it does not lighten it. Healthy skin in your own tone is the goal.
  • Skin change runs on a 4–12 week clock. Anything promising days is selling, not informing.
