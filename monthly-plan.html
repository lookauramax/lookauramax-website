November 2026

This month's plan: November 2026

November is the busiest grooming month of the Indian year: Diwali on Sunday 8 November, Movember running all month, peak wedding season opening, and — across north India — the air turning cooler and drier, which changes what your skin needs. One focus trait carries the month: skin. The weeks below assume a routine you already have, or are starting; none of it needs new products to begin.

Before the month — the haircut window

Thu 29 October – Sun 1 November

Book the haircut in this window. A cut taken here has a week to grow in and look natural by Diwali, instead of looking day-one sharp in every festival photo. Starting a 90-day Aura Cycle this month? Take your Day 1 baseline photos this weekend too — a face photo, plus a guided side photo for posture — in the light you can repeat at each check-in.

Week 1 — Diwali week

Mon 2 – Sun 8 November · Focus: Skin

Festival weeks punish routines: late nights, rich food, more time outdoors. Keep the routine boring and steady — cleanser, moisturiser, SPF in the morning; cleanse again before bed, however late. Diwali is Sunday 8 November — the day most photos of you will be taken this month, so the week's job is simply not to arrive at it with a week of skipped basics. Smoke and city air that evening make the bedtime cleanse the one step not to skip.

Week 2 — Movember: beard and moustache care

Mon 9 – Sun 15 November · Focus: Beard

November is Movember — the month men grow a moustache to start conversations about men's health. Whether you are growing one, keeping your beard, or clean-shaven, this week's work is care, not growth promises: trim the lines once, wash the beard with the same gentleness as your face, and keep the skin under it moisturised. Post-festival, put sleep back on schedule — it is the input every other trait reads.

Week 3 — Wedding-season hair

Mon 16 – Sun 22 November · Focus: Hair

Peak wedding season is open, and event photos are unforgiving about neglected hair. If your last cut was in the October window, this week is tidy-up time: neckline, edges, and the shape — not a restyle. Wash-day rhythm matters more than product: hair washed the day before an event sits better than hair washed an hour before. Our wedding season glow-up guide covers guest and groom preparation in full.

Week 4 — The winter skin shift, and the month review

Mon 23 – Sun 30 November · Focus: Skin

Across north India the air is cooler and drier now; skin that was fine in October starts to feel tight after washing. Shift one step heavier on moisturiser, keep showers lukewarm rather than hot, and do not add three new products in the same week — one change at a time, so you can tell what helped. Monday 30 November is the month review: if your Aura Cycle started on 1 November, this is your Day 30 Aura Check-in — re-scan under the same light and compare bands, not feelings.

Guides for this month: the Diwali grooming guide, our Movember guide, and the winter skincare guide for men. The free 90-Day Planner turns any start date into dated check-ins.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good monthly grooming plan for men?

One focus trait for the month, a weekly rhythm you can actually keep, and one honest review at the end of the month. That is the shape of the plan on this page: November 2026 runs Diwali week first (skin), then Movember beard and moustache care, wedding-season hair, and a winter skin shift to close — each week dated, each with a small set of actions.

What is this month's grooming plan?

For November 2026: get your haircut between Thu 29 October and Sun 1 November so it grows in by Diwali (Sun 8 November). Week 1 (2–8 Nov) is Diwali week — keep your skin routine steady through late nights. Week 2 (9–15 Nov) is Movember beard and moustache care plus a sleep reset. Week 3 (16–22 Nov) is wedding-season hair. Week 4 (23–30 Nov) shifts skin care for cooler, drier air and closes with a month review on Mon 30 November.

How does a month fit inside the 90-day Aura Cycle?

A 90-day Aura Cycle is three monthly blocks. Month 1 (Days 1–30) is Foundation: baseline scan, core routine, first Aura Check-in on Day 30. Month 2 (Days 31–60) is Building: the routine widens around what the first check-in showed, second check-in on Day 60. Month 3 (Days 61–90) is Refining: small adjustments, final check-in and cycle review on Day 90. This page's dated monthly plan is the calendar layer on top of whichever month of the cycle you are in.

Is the scan really free?

Yes. The AuraScan itself is free — no plan and no card. A free scan shows every signal name and the band it earned, plus your #1 finding in full, Why and Action included. Plans unlock the full report, the Aura Plan, and the Coach. There is no permanent free plan; the free scan is the free part.
